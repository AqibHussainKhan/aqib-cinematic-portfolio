## Final generation prompt — built-in imagegen

Use case: identity-preserve. Asset type: one transparent 12-frame character turntable sprite sheet used as a rotating website portrait texture. Input image 1 is the identity and wardrobe reference. Preserve this exact young South Asian man's recognizable face, almond-shaped brown eyes, thick dark eyebrows, thick wavy/curly black hair, mustache, short trimmed beard, warm natural complexion, beige blazer and open-collar black shirt. No sunglasses, no white kurta. Keep his natural expression. Remove the room, desk, laptop and chair. Photorealistic studio bust, from top of hair to waist, arms relaxed outside the central chest area. Produce ONE SQUARE transparent PNG, ideally 2048x2048, in precisely FOUR EQUAL COLUMNS and THREE EQUAL ROWS. All 12 cells have the same crop, head height, scale, lighting and subject center. Each bust stays inside its own cell, entire head visible with a little clear space above, body cropped flush at each cell bottom. No labels, text, cell borders or backgrounds. True alpha transparency. Soft neutral lighting, realistic fabric, consistent jacket lapels and black shirt. Read frames left to right, row by row: cell0 FRONT 0 degrees, cell1 facing toward image RIGHT 30 degrees, cell2 facing toward image RIGHT 60 degrees, cell3 full profile looking RIGHT 90 degrees; cell4 showing back and right shoulder 120 degrees with little face visible, cell5 near back 150 degrees, cell6 directly BACK 180 degrees with back of head and jacket and NO face visible, cell7 back and opposite shoulder 210 degrees; cell8 opposite rear three-quarter 240 degrees, cell9 full profile looking LEFT 270 degrees, cell10 looking LEFT 300 degrees, cell11 almost front with face angled slightly LEFT 330 degrees. The last row MUST show the OPPOSITE SIDE of the face from the first row. This is a real continuous 360-degree turntable. Fixed camera. Turn head, chest and shoulders together. No decorative layout, no glow, no ground, no contact shadows. Keep the person's identity strongly matched to the reference photo, especially his face and eyes. High-quality natural photography.

The tool returned 1254 × 1254 pixels. The application computes cell sizes from the actual image dimensions.
